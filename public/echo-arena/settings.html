Game Pass plans

What a pass costs and how long it lasts. The checkout contract is the authority on price and duration — buyPass asserts the treasury received exactly its own price, so a row here that disagrees does not re-price anything, it makes the buyer's transaction revert after they have already paid gas to approve. Change the contract with setPlan() first, this table second.
