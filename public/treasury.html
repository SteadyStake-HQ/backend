Relayer wallet & fees

One wallet does two jobs on every network: it pays the native gas for each auto-execution, and it receives the fee that execution charges the user's gas tank. The two are different tokens, so the wallet can be collecting fees steadily and still be minutes from being unable to execute — this page shows both sides, per network, and the margin between them.
